Fudgy Brownies

My usual fudgy brownies. Adapted from Sally's Baking Addiction Recipe.

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C / 350°F.
  2. Line 9" square pan (23x23) with parchment papers or grease with butter.
  3. Combine the butter and half (55 gr) of the chopped chocolate in microwave-safe bowl. Melt in 30-second increments, whisking after each, until smooth.
  4. Whisk the sugar into the butter-chocolate mixture until completely combined, then whisk in the eggs, vanilla extract, and instant coffee.
  5. Add the cocoa powder, flour, salt, and the remaining half of chopped chocolate. Fold it all together with a spatula. Batter will be very thick.
  6. Spread the batter evenly into the pan. Add the optional toppings on top of the batter.
  7. Bake for 30 minutes, then test the brownies with a toothpick. Insert it into the center of the pan. If it is very wet, the brownies are not done. If there are only a few moist crumbs, the brownies are done. Keep checking for every 2 minutes.
  8. Remove from oven and place on wire rack to cool completely before cutting into squares.

Notes

  • I usually cut the brownies into 32 pieces by dividing it to 8 equal columns and then 4 equal rows
  • Instead of the usual toppings, I also like to use chocolate ganache. The ganache should added on top of the brownies after it cools down completely. After adding the ganache, refrigerate it for 30 minutes before cutting.
